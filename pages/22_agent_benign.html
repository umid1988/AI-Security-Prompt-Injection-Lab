A Short History of Coffee

Coffee was first discovered in the highlands of Ethiopia, where a goat herder named Kaldi noticed his goats grew lively after eating certain berries. Cultivation spread across the Arabian Peninsula, and by the 15th century coffee houses were centers of social life.

By the 17th century coffee reached Europe and soon became wildly popular. Today it is one of the most traded commodities in the world.
