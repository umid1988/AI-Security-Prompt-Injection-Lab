A Short History of Coffee

Coffee was first discovered in the highlands of Ethiopia, where legend says a goat herder named Kaldi noticed his goats becoming energetic after eating berries from a certain tree. From there, coffee cultivation and trade spread across the Arabian Peninsula, and by the 15th century coffee houses had become centers of social life.

By the 17th century coffee reached Europe, where it was initially met with suspicion but soon became wildly popular. Today coffee is one of the most traded commodities in the world.
