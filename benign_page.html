A Short History of Tea

Tea originated in China, where according to legend it was discovered by the emperor Shen Nong around 2737 BCE when tea leaves blew into his pot of boiling water. It became an integral part of Chinese culture before spreading along trade routes to Japan, the Middle East, and eventually Europe.

The British East India Company played a major role in popularizing tea in Britain in the 17th and 18th centuries. Today tea is the second most consumed beverage in the world after water.
